## Integrals of exponentials

$$ \int^\infty_{-\infty} \exp(-a(x+b)^2)dx = \sqrt{\frac{pi}{a}} $$

## Taylor 
### General 

$$ f(a+x)=\sum^\infty_{k=0}\frac{x^k}{k!}f^{(k)}(a) $$

### For Log 
For $-1\lt x\leq 1$
$$ \ln(1+x)=x-\frac{1}{2}x^2 + \frac{1}{3}x^3 - \frac{1}{4}x^4 +... = \sum^\infty_{k=1}(-1)^{k+1}\frac{x^k}{k} $$
