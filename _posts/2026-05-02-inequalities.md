## Hoeffding's

Let $X_1,...,X_n$ be independent r.v s.t $X_i$ takes values in $[a_i, b_i]$ almost surely $\forall i\leq n$. Let $$S = \sum^n_{i=1}(X_i-\mathbb{E}[X_i])$$

Then $$\forall t>0$, $$\mathbb{P}\{S\geq t\}\leq \exp\Big(-\frac{2t^2}{\sum^n_{i=1}(b_i-a_i)^2}\Big)$$
